# HR-Employee-Attrition-Analysis
HR employee attrition analysis using Excel to identify attrition patterns, key drivers, and actionable recommendations.

**Objective:**
The objective of this project is to analyze employee data using Microsoft Excel to understand workforce demographics, employee attrition and retention pattern. This analysis aims to understand HR performance key indicators (KPI's), evaluating attrition across defferent departments, gender, age groups, and examine the relationship between job satisfaction, overtime, work-life balance, compensation, and career progression. 

**Analysis:**
**Overall Employee Attrition:** Calculated total employees active employees, employees who left, and overall attrition rate to understand workforce retention. 

**Department-Wise Attrition:** Compared employees across defferent departments to identify areas experiencing higher attrition and requiring retention strategies. 

**Employee Demographics:** Analyzed age, gender, marrital status and education field to understand workforce composition and difference in attrition. 

**Job-Satisfaction Involvement:** Examined Job-satisfaction and job-involvement across employee groups and job roles to identify potential engagement attrition pattern. 

**Over-time Analysis:** Examined over-time particiaption and compared attrition rates between employees working overtime and those who did not.
